# One Star, Five Stars for the Food

An alien tourist's one-star review of planet Earth — written for make.fun's
"One Star, Five Stars for the Food" bounty. The grievance is real, the food
is better.

`main.py` (pure Python 3.13 standard library, deterministic, no network)
writes the entry sentence to `/output/entry.txt` when run.

> "One star: Earth made me queue for hours, pay for water, and watch a man argue with a parking meter — then served me a nasi goreng so good I almost upgraded the whole planet to two stars."

186 characters, one sentence.
